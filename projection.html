Projection notes

    C4 coverage is held level. Original C4 rates remain level for the product's named initial term, then move to the shared C4 subsequent table. Subsequent blocks are held for the number of years in that table; age 99 is the final premium year and the policy is shown as expired in the year the insured turns 100.
    For an eligible DT100 conversion, the first subsequent year's premium and coverage stay unchanged. Beginning the following year, that premium remains level while coverage is calculated from the C4 DT100 face-amount table using basic annual premium before the $75 policy fee.
    Custom Exchange coverage remains level for policy years 1–10, then automatically decreases using the policy's issue-age column from the Custom Exchange death-benefit table. Its base premium and policy fee remain level.
    When Custom Exchange is converted to ART at policy year 11, its original coverage remains level and its premium changes annually using attained-age ART rates. The current effective class, combined coverage band, table rating, billing mode, and applicable $75 base-policy fee are used.
    Other product families and riders remain visible but are marked Rate unavailable until their projection rules are added. Current combined coverage bands and table ratings are held constant throughout this version.
    * Partial total: excludes an unavailable premium. † Waiver is omitted because its subsequent-period calculation is intentionally not projected. During an original level period, available waiver premium is removed beginning with age 60.
